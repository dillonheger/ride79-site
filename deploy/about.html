RIDE79 is an independent, owner-run 79BIKE dealer based in London. It exists for one reason: riders who want serious 72V performance shouldn't have to choose between paying for a famous badge and buying from a factory with no one in the UK to call.

THE STORY

Why RIDE79
exists.

Look at the UK electric dirt bike market and you see the same pattern. Most riders end up on the same couple of brands and pay for the name. The alternative is ordering from an overseas factory website, and if something goes wrong a year later there's nobody local to talk to.

79BIKE's spec sheet didn't fit that pattern. Both Falcons use a 72V platform and 79BIKE's own motor, with hardware like Fastace suspension, NFC start and, on the GT, E-ABS. Both cost well under what the obvious names charge. What was missing was someone in the UK standing behind them. That's RIDE79.

RIDE79 is small on purpose. It's owner-run, so the person who answers your WhatsApp is the person who sold you the bike and the person who chases your warranty claim. There's no call centre and no ticket queue. If you need a part, a fix or a straight answer, you know who to ask.
